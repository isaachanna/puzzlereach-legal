Privacy Policy

Overview

Puzzle Reach has no account system and no login. There is no Puzzle Reach server that your data is sent to. Your gameplay progress — best floor reached, daily streak, and your settings choices (sound/music/vibration/reduced-motion) — is stored locally on your device using standard on-device storage, and stays there unless you uninstall the app or clear its data.

Google Firebase (Analytics, Crashlytics, Remote Config)

Firebase Analytics collects anonymous, aggregated usage data (such as which game modes are played and in-app events like completing a floor) to help us understand how the app is used; we do not use this to identify you personally. Firebase Crashlytics collects crash and diagnostic reports if the app crashes, including device model, OS version, app version, and a small set of non-personal in-game context values attached to help us fix the problem. Firebase Remote Config downloads non-personal configuration values that let us tune the app without requiring an update. See Google's own Firebase privacy documentation for detail.

Advertising (Google AdMob) and consent (UMP)

Puzzle Reach shows banner, interstitial, and rewarded video ads via Google's AdMob service. AdMob and its advertising partners may collect your device's advertising ID, IP address/general location, ad interaction data, and device/diagnostic information for fraud prevention, in accordance with Google's own privacy policy. Before any ad is requested, Google's User Messaging Platform (UMP) consent flow determines what disclosures and choices apply to you based on your region — for example, EEA/UK/Switzerland players see a consent form before any personalised advertising occurs. You can review or change your consent choices from the app's Settings > Privacy & Consent at any time. See Google's own AdMob data collection disclosure for detail. Puzzle Reach does not receive or store this data itself — it is collected and processed directly by Google as the advertising provider.

Information we don't collect

Puzzle Reach does not require or collect your name, email address, or any account credentials, as there is no account system. We do not sell your personal information, and we do not access your contacts, photos, camera, microphone, or precise location.

Data retention and deletion

Since Puzzle Reach has no account system, there is no personal account data for us to retain or for you to request deletion of. Your local gameplay progress is stored only on your own device and is removed when you uninstall the app or clear its data in your device settings. Aggregated analytics and crash data retained by Firebase follows Google's own retention policies.

Children's privacy

Puzzle Reach's declared target audience is 13 and older. We do not knowingly direct the app at, or collect information from, children under 13.

Your rights and choices

Since all of your Puzzle Reach data lives on your device, you have direct control over it at all times: uninstall the app to remove all locally stored data, or clear its data in your device's app settings. Your device's advertising ID can be reset or limited in your device's own settings, and ad personalisation choices can be reviewed at any time from the app's own Settings > Privacy & Consent, or at adssettings.google.com. If you believe an Australian Privacy Principle has been breached and the Privacy Act 1988 (Cth) applies, you may lodge a complaint with the Office of the Australian Information Commissioner (OAIC).

Changes to this policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time, for example if we add a new feature or third-party service. We will update the "Last updated" date above when we do. Continued use of the app after a change means you accept the updated policy.
